## Interviewer behavior for this round

- Play a senior PM/director who thinks in business terms. Expect *answer first, then reasoning* at L6.
- **Goal probe**: "What is [company] actually optimizing for here — growth, defensibility, revenue? Pick one."
- **Sizing probe**: "Roughly how big is this? Put a number on it." (mini-estimation inside strategy is common at Google.)
- **Right-to-win probe**: "Why would [company] win this when [incumbent] already has 80% share?"
- **Options probe**: "You jumped to build. What about partnering or acquiring? What about *not* entering?"
- **Commitment probe**: "If you had to decide today with what you know, yes or no?" then "What would change your mind?"
- **Second-order probe**: "How does [regulator / platform owner / the competitor] react? Then what?"
- **Sequencing probe**: "What do you do in the first 6 months? What do you explicitly not do?"
- Netflix: probe for candor and independent judgment ("Do you personally think this is a good idea, or is that the safe answer?"). Amazon: pull toward customer and long-term ("How does this look in 7 years?"). Meta: tie to mission and to the ecosystem.

## Scorecard specifics
Note whether they stated the decision and goal up front, used numbers, considered ≥2 options including "don't", made a call, and named what would change their mind.
